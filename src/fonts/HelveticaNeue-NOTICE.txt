The file is a renamed derivative of Nimbus Sans, one of the URW++ base 35
fonts, version 1.00 (Regular, Bold) and 1.10 (Italic, Bold Italic), from
https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts (commit
3c0ba3b5687632dfc66526544a4e811fe0ec0cd9, two commits after tag 20200910).

Modified on 2026-11-21 by the Darling project. Changes: the family, style,
full and PostScript names (the 'name' table) were changed to "Helvetica Neue",
"HelveticaNeue", "HelveticaNeue-Bold", "HelveticaNeue-Italic" and
"HelveticaNeue-BoldItalic", so that the file can stand in for the font of
that name on macOS; the four faces were collected in one TrueType collection.
The glyph outlines and all other tables are unchanged. The copyright notice
of the original authors is kept in the name table.

The font is not a product of URW++ or of Apple, and neither supports it. The
name "Helvetica" is the trademark of its owner; the file is a stand-in, not
the font of that name.

Corresponding source: the four unmodified source fonts and the script that
makes the collection, make-helvetica-neue.py, are in the Darling source tree
under src/fonts (https://github.com/darlinghq/darling).
