System sounds of the Darling prefix
===================================

The 14 files are converted copies of 14 files of the "Interface Sounds" pack by Kenney
(<https://www.kenney.nl>, <https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds>), released under
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (public domain dedication), see LICENSE-Kenney-Interface-Sounds.txt.
Conversion: ffmpeg, to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit big-endian PCM in an AIFF container, metadata
removed; no other processing.

The file names are the names of the sounds that +[NSSound soundNamed:] looks up; the sounds are
not related to any other system's sounds.
